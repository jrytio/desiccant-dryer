Every state the screen can show

One layout, twelve situations. Sanity-check the language and the colors: is anything misleading, missing, or too subtle? Same 8-bit palette as before. Shown at about 1.4×.

1. Starting

Dryer just enabled; first control tick has not run. Nothing lit, no roles.

2. A in service, B wet

Normal running before breakthrough. B waits at ambient; its timer counts time since the swap.

3. A in service, B heating

RH crossed arm (5%). Heater B on, gauge in the amber zone, timer is heater run time.

4. A in service, B cooling

Regen done, heater off. Fill still orange until it drops under cooldown (40°). Timer is time cooling.

5. A in service, B ready

Fill back to blue, READY in blue, IN SERVICE in green. Swap fires when RH reaches the swap line (10%).

6. Over the limit, B not ready

RH past the swap line while B is still heating: red zone lit, OVER, status says waiting.

7. Near the limit (HIGH)

Upper third of the amber zone: B is cooling, swap will fire as soon as it is ready and RH reaches the line.

8. B in service, A heating

The mirror case after a swap. Lit path and open valve move to B; A's heater is on its outer side.

9. Fault: standby overtemp

Strip turns red. Faulted pack outlined red with FAULT in place of its phase. Heater off, air keeps flowing through A.

10. Disabled

Dryer Enabled off. Nothing lit, both valves shown closed, phases read OFF. Temperatures stay live.

11. Boot (first minute)

Same as normal but the strip shows the IP address for 60 s after boot, then reverts.

12. Probe B missing, simulating RH

A NaN probe shows --° and no fill; the heater is blocked by the controller. SIM badge because the RH is the slider.
